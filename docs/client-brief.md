# Website Development Brief — Business Support Agency

## 1. Website Overview

Create a modern, professional, minimalistic website for our U.S.-led remote business support agency.

We provide three core services:

- Administrative Support
- Data Analytics & Reporting
- Video Editing & Creative Support

We work with businesses, entrepreneurs, and content creators who want to delegate work, improve efficiency, and focus on growth.

Our business is U.S.-led and works with skilled remote professionals, including team members in the Philippines.

*Reference:* https://www.bruntwork.co/

We want the same level of professionalism, but a cleaner, simpler, and more distinctive website.

*Only three pages:*
1. Home
2. Portfolio
3. Contact Us

## 2. Design Direction

The website should resemble a premium U.S. business consulting or creative services agency, not a generic outsourcing company.

Design preferences:
- Modern, minimalist, professional
- Navy blue, charcoal, white, and subtle accent colors
- Large headlines with clean typography
- Generous whitespace
- Smooth scrolling and subtle animations
- High-quality visuals
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- Professional but approachable messaging

Avoid excessive stock photos, cluttered layouts, and generic outsourcing language.

- Additional pages
- Complicated pricing calculators
- Public pricing for now
- Generic VA agency messaging
- Excessive animations
- Fake testimonials or invented statistics
- Overly long paragraphs
- A crowded homepage

## 8. Main Website Objective

We want to be recognized as a reliable business support partner, not simply an inexpensive outsourcing company.

The website must communicate three benefits:

*We handle your operations. We make sense of your data. We elevate your content.*

The goal is to turn visitors into clients through a professional presentation, real work samples, and an easy contact process.
